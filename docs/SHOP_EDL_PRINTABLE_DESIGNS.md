## Shop-level public signals (manual observation)

Observed on the live shop page (not from private analytics):

- Badge / trust: ~**28.5k sales**, **5.0** from ~**2.8k reviews**, **~3 years** on Etsy.
- Catalog size shown as **~1111 items**; sidebar sections emphasize **clipart** and **digital paper**.
- Example sections: Watercolor / Animal / Floral / People / Landscape / Cute / Food / Dress / Christmas / Halloween clipart.
- Storefront presentation: strong brand banner, all visible cards marked **Digital Download**, frequent **sale %** badges.
- Product type skew: primarily **PNG/JPG clipart + seamless digital paper**, not framed wall-poster printables.

## What appears to “work” here (hypotheses)

1. **High SKU volume** of related digital assets (clipart packs + seamless papers).
2. **Clear digital-download labeling** on every card.
3. **Seasonal + evergreen mix** across clipart categories.
4. Recognizable watercolor / cute illustration system.
5. For *our* poster generator: borrow theme clusters only — do not copy assets.
