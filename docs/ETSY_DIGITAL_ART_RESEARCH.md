## Part A — Exact research prompt (reusable brief)

### Purpose

Use this document as a reusable research brief for finding Etsy opportunities for digital art and printable posters only.

The goal is to discover products that customers download themselves. Do not research physical posters, print-on-demand, framed art, canvases, shipping, packaging, fulfillment, or delivery.

### Core instruction

Research Etsy for digital-download wall art and poster products. Focus on niches, visual styles, keywords, formats, price ranges, bundles, and listing patterns that could help create original digital artwork for an Etsy shop.

Only include products that satisfy all or most of these conditions:

- The customer receives a digital file or files.
- No physical item is mailed.
- No seller-side printing is required.
- No shipping or delivery logistics are involved.
- The artwork can be created as an original design or substantially edited original composition.
- The product is suitable for home décor, office décor, nursery décor, gallery walls, gifts, or personal printing.

### Important exclusions

Exclude results containing or primarily focused on:

- Physical posters or prints
- Print-on-demand products
- Framed prints
- Canvas prints
- Metal prints
- Posters shipped by the seller
- Shipping, postage, packaging, dispatch, or delivery times
- Fulfillment companies
- Wholesale physical inventory
- Copyrighted characters, celebrities, sports teams, logos, movie art, album covers, or trademarked slogans
- AI prompt packs sold as the product
- Generic advice unrelated to digital wall art

If a search result mixes digital and physical products, keep only the digital-download information and label the result as mixed or uncertain.

### Research goals

Find and compare:

- Digital poster niches with visible buyer interest.
- Styles that appear repeatedly across successful listings.
- Search phrases buyers may use.
- Common file formats and aspect ratios.
- Typical prices for single prints and bundles.
- Whether listings are instant downloads or made-to-order digital files.
- What is included in each product, such as JPG, PNG, PDF, multiple ratios, or size guide.
- How sellers explain that no physical item is shipped.
- Common weaknesses that create an opportunity for a better product.
- Copyright, trademark, and AI-disclosure risks.

Treat these as research categories, not instructions to copy existing sellers.

### How to evaluate demand without overclaiming

Use observable signals rather than pretending to know exact sales data:

- Number and quality of relevant search results.
- Reviews and review recency where publicly visible.
- Repeated appearance of a niche across Etsy search pages.
- Multiple shops offering related products.
- Clear buyer use cases.
- Coherent product bundles and consistent visual styles.
- Signs that listings are actively maintained.
- Whether the niche has room for a distinct angle.

Do not state that an item is guaranteed to sell. Use labels such as promising, worth testing, crowded, uncertain, or needs more validation.

### Product strategy

Prefer a focused collection instead of unrelated one-off images. A practical first collection might contain:

- One clear visual style.
- One primary audience or room.
- 10–20 related designs.
- A few single-print listings.
- A few coordinated bundles.
- Matching color palettes and mockup presentation.
- Multiple print ratios where practical.

Good differentiation may include:

- A recognizable original visual system.
- Better color harmony and typography.
- A coherent three-, six-, or nine-print gallery-wall set.
- Carefully prepared files in common ratios.
- A clear size guide.
- A useful buyer instruction PDF.
- Personalization that produces an original digital file.
- A narrow audience that is underserved.

### Digital-file requirements to investigate

For every product idea, determine what a buyer actually needs to print it. Investigate whether the listing includes:

- High-resolution JPG or PNG files.
- PDF files when appropriate.
- Several aspect ratios, such as 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, and international paper sizes.
- A ZIP file when several files are included.
- A printable size guide.
- A note explaining that the buyer downloads and prints the file independently.
- Instructions for home printing, a local print shop, or an online print service.
- A clear statement that no physical product is shipped.

Do not promise a specific resolution, color profile, file limit, or Etsy upload limit unless verified from current official Etsy documentation.

### Etsy listing outline

For each final product, prepare:

**Title** — Use natural buyer language. Put the main product phrase near the beginning, but do not stuff the title with repetitive keywords.

Example structure:

`Printable [subject] Wall Art, [style] Digital Poster, [room/use], Instant Download`

**Description** — Include:

- What the buyer receives.
- Number and type of files.
- Available ratios or sizes.
- How the download works.
- That this is a digital product only.
- That no physical item will be shipped.
- Printing suggestions.
- Personal-use or commercial-use terms.
- Refund/download clarification consistent with current Etsy rules.
- AI disclosure if AI tools were used.

**Tags and attributes** — Use only relevant phrases. Build tags from the actual design, subject, style, room, audience, format, and use case. Avoid unrelated popular terms.

**Images** — Use listing images that show:

- The artwork clearly.
- A room mockup for scale and context.
- A preview of included ratios or sizes.
- A “digital download only” notice.
- A sample of the buyer instruction sheet.
- A bundle overview when multiple files are included.

Do not use mockups that imply the customer receives a framed or printed item unless the listing clearly identifies them as display previews.

### AI and originality checklist

If AI tools are used:

- Use original prompts and develop a distinct concept.
- Edit, curate, color-correct, crop, or compose the result where appropriate.
- Inspect hands, faces, lettering, borders, and artifacts.
- Never copy another Etsy seller’s composition, title, tags, or mockups.
- Avoid recognizable copyrighted characters, logos, brands, celebrity likenesses, and protected designs.
- Check the image-generator’s commercial-use terms.
- Disclose AI use in the Etsy listing where required by Etsy policy.
- Do not sell the prompt as a substitute for the artwork unless that is separately permitted and accurately described.

Etsy’s Seller Handbook says sellers may use their original prompts with AI tools to create artwork, but AI use must be disclosed in the listing description. Verify the current policy before publishing because marketplace rules can change. Source: [https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004)

### Final research output

Return the research in this order:

1. Five to ten promising digital-only poster niches.
2. A comparison table using the fields above.
3. The best three niches to test first, with reasons.
4. Ten original product concepts for the selected niche.
5. Suggested Etsy search phrases for each concept.
6. A digital-file package for each concept.
7. A draft title, description, and tags for the strongest concept.
8. A copyright, trademark, and AI-policy risk check.
9. A seven-day validation plan using Etsy search and listing data.
10. A short list of claims that could not be verified.

### Strict final filter

Before recommending anything, ask:

- Is this digital-only?
- Does the customer download it?
- Is there no physical copy or seller delivery?
- Can the design be made original?
- Is the buyer use case clear?
- Is the keyword specific enough to test?
- Have copyright and trademark risks been checked?
- Has AI use been disclosed when applicable?

If the answer to any important question is no or unknown, mark the idea **needs verification** rather than presenting it as ready to publish.

### 3. Best three niches to test first

1. **Soft storybook nursery animal printable sets** — Clear buyer use case (nursery / gift), natural bundle AOV, maps cleanly to coordinated digital files, low shipping complexity. Competition is high → win with a tight palette, consistent characters, and excellent mockups. **Worth testing.**
2. **Warm-neutral botanical gallery wall printables** — Evergreen décor intent; buyers often want matching sets. Differentiation via a recognizable color system and ratio pack quality. **Worth testing.**
3. **Japandi / calm Scandinavian printable sets** — Narrower than generic minimalist; aligns with warm-neutral trend shift reported for 2026. **Worth testing / needs more validation** on exact keyword competition.

Motivational typography and generic abstract are deferred: demand exists but differentiation and price pressure look harder for a new shop.

### 6. Digital-file package (standard for each concept)

Recommend one consistent package (verify Etsy upload limits before publishing):

- High-resolution **JPG** (sRGB) for each ratio
- Optional **PNG** if transparency is needed (usually not for posters)
- Optional **PDF** size guide + buyer print instructions
- Delivered as **ZIP** when multiple files
- Ratios to prioritize: **2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 11:14, ISO/A-series** (common seller pattern; pixel targets e.g. 18×24" @ 300 DPI = 5400×7200 for 3:4)
- Listing copy must state: **digital download only; no physical item shipped**
- Instant download for premade designs; custom name art may be **made-to-order digital** with clear turnaround
